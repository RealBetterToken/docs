Release date: September 22, 2026

OpenAI adds Sol and Luna alongside Astra in the GPT-6 family. Sol targets complex coding and agentic workflows; Luna is designed for focused tasks at high volume. OpenAI reports improvements over the GPT-5.6 generation in coding, factuality, and communication.

BetterToken now supports **GPT-6 Sol** and **GPT-6 Luna**. Select the `GPT` provider, set the Base URL to `{{openai-base-url}}`, and use `gpt-6-sol` or `gpt-6-luna`. The official model name is **Sol**, not `Sora`.

Check the <a href={"{{model-plaza-url}}"}>model plaza</a> for current BetterToken availability and pricing.

## Which model to choose

- **`gpt-6-sol`**: for complex coding and agentic workflows.
- **`gpt-6-luna`**: for focused, repeatable, high-volume tasks.

## Highlights

- **Large context**: OpenAI lists a `1,050,000`-token context window and up to `128,000` output tokens for both models.
- **Adjustable reasoning**: both support `none`, `low`, `medium`, `high`, `xhigh`, and `max` reasoning effort.
- **Everyday work**: OpenAI reports gains over GPT-5.6 in coding, factual accuracy, and clearer technical responses.
